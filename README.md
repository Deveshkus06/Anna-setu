# Anna-Setu

> Bridging surplus food to those who need it most.

Anna-Setu is an AI-powered food redistribution platform that connects institutional canteens, university messes, and corporate cafeterias with nearby orphanages and NGO distribution hubs. It helps turn surplus cooked food into safe, timely meals through smart matching, predictive analytics, and optimized delivery routes.

## The Problem

Large quantities of food are wasted daily by institutional kitchens because meal turnout is difficult to predict. At the same time, nearby communities face food insecurity.

Current redistribution is often slow and manual, which creates challenges such as:

- Lack of real-time visibility into surplus food availability
- Limited time before cooked food loses freshness
- Inefficient matching between donors and recipients
- Increased environmental impact from food waste

## Our Solution

Anna-Setu provides a hyper-local logistics network for surplus cooked food.

1. Canteen staff log available surplus meals.
2. The platform estimates food shelf-life and packaging requirements.
3. AI matches the donation with nearby verified orphanages or NGOs.
4. Smart routing assigns the fastest available delivery route.
5. Recipients receive notifications, confirm delivery, and maintain digital quality logs.

## Key Features

- **AI Yield Prediction**  
  Predicts meal turnout using historical schedules and weather patterns to reduce over-cooking.

- **Smart Auto-Matching**  
  Matches surplus food with nearby verified recipient organizations based on distance and capacity.

- **Dynamic Route Optimization**  
  Generates efficient delivery routes for volunteers and logistics partners.

- **Donor Portal**  
  Lets mess and canteen staff post surplus meal counts through a simple mobile-friendly interface.

- **Recipient & NGO Interface**  
  Sends instant alerts to recipients and supports food acceptance, delivery confirmation, and digital records.

- **Food Safety Tracking**  
  Tracks preparation time, shelf-life windows, packaging requirements, and delivery verification.

## Impact Goals

Anna-Setu aims to:

- Reduce unrecovered institutional food waste
- Deliver surplus meals within safe consumption windows
- Support local orphanages, NGOs, and vulnerable communities
- Reduce landfill waste and related emissions
- Build a scalable, technology-enabled food redistribution network
